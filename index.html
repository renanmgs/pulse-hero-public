Launch preparation

Pulse Hero is being prepared for public release on mobile stores. This site hosts the public product page, privacy policy, and account deletion information required for store review.

The daily loop

Movement becomes progress without turning training into homework.

Connect activity

Optional Health Connect and Apple Health access lets steps, workouts, distance, calories, and heart rate count when you train.

Claim RPG rewards

Validated movement can grant XP, gold, materials, encounters, and quest progress after server-side checks.

Build your hero

Spend progression on classes, talents, gear, runes, crafting, and a combat deck that fits how you play.

Train with people

Friends, teams, trades, and duels connect real activity to social RPG goals without requiring constant screen time.

Core systems

Built for quick sessions, with RPG depth when you want it.

Workouts and ambient steps

Start dedicated workouts, import supported health sessions, or sync day-to-day steps for lighter progress.

Turn-based combat

Encounters, dungeons, campaign scenes, skills, talents, and gear feed a tactical RPG layer.

Classes and builds

Unlock class paths through physical challenges, then swap builds as your training goals change.

Privacy-first controls

Health, location, camera, and notification permissions are optional and controlled by your device settings.
