The daily loop

Movement becomes progress without turning training into homework.

Connect activity

Optional Health Connect or Apple Health access lets steps, distance, workouts, calories and heart rate count when you train. You can also log a workout in the app and skip the sync entirely.

Claim RPG rewards

Validated movement grants XP, gold, materials, encounters and quest progress. Every credit is checked server-side against plausibility caps, so a leaderboard is worth something.

Build your hero

Spend progression on 13 classes, gear, runes, professions and a four-skill combat deck. Each class has its own passive, signature and skill ladder.

Train with people

Friends, groups, trading and hero-vs-hero duels connect real activity to social goals — without asking you to keep the app open all day.

From the app

Train, evolve, battle.

Core systems

Built for quick sessions, with RPG depth when you want it.

Workouts and ambient steps

Record a workout from a plan or free-form, import a supported health session, or just let the day's steps sync for lighter progress. A rules-based coach adapts the plan to what you actually did.

Turn-based combat

Campaign scenes, dungeons, an endless tower and random encounters, all fought with a deck of class skills, statuses, items and gear.

Classes and builds

Thirteen flat classes — no starter tax, no locked tiers. Unlock them through physical challenges and swap builds whenever your training goals change.

Professions and crafting

Mine, forage and hunt for materials, then feed six crafting professions: armorsmith, weaponsmith, leatherworker, tailor, alchemist and runecrafter.

Social and PvP

Friends, groups, trading, hero-vs-hero duels and a weekly arena league that ranks builds instead of reflexes.

Before you install

Straight answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Pulse Hero is free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases (a premium currency and a monthly premium subscription for the fitness planning and analysis features), plus an optional season pass.

Are there ads?

Only rewarded ones, and only if you choose to watch them. There are no forced interstitials, no banners and no ads on app open — a rewarded video is something you opt into for a specific reward, never something that interrupts you.

Do I have to connect a health app?

No. Health Connect on Android and Apple Health on iOS are optional. Without them you can still record workouts directly in the app. If you do connect, access is read-only — Pulse Hero does not write anything back to your health record.

What health data does it read?

Steps, distance (walking/running, cycling, swimming), active and total calories, heart rate, and workout sessions. It is used for your own fitness progress and game progression. It is not used for advertising and not sold. The privacy policy has the full list.

Can I cheat by shaking my phone?

Not usefully. Activity is ingested by the game server and validated against caps and plausibility checks before anything is credited, so the arena and the leagues rank training rather than wrist technique.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Pulse Hero needs a connection to play: the game server is the authority on your hero, and activity is validated and credited server-side rather than on the device.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 15 or later, and Android 8.0 or later. The app is available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.

How do I delete my account?

From inside the app, or by request. The steps and what gets erased are on the account deletion page.
